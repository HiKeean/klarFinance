Backend schema reference — single diagram

klarFinance Data Model

All 31 tables across all 8 backend schemas (auth, dbo, los, fin, qris, referral, transjakarta, geo) in one combined entity-relationship diagram — drawn directly from the live @Entity classes, not from documentation.

Diagram is wide — scroll horizontally inside its frame, or zoom out, to see it in full on one screen.

|| = exactly one |o = zero or one o{ = zero or many |{ = one or many mark sits next to the entity it describes — e.g. Loan ||--o{ Installment reads "one Loan has zero-or-many Installments"

Kegunaan tiap tabel

Fungsi dari masing-masing 31 tabel di atas, dikelompokkan per schema.

auth — identitas, RBAC & sesi login
User
Akun login utama (nasabah maupun staff internal) — menyimpan identity, password, role, dan status soft-delete.
Role
Daftar peran (NASABAH, CHECKER, BM, SUPERADMIN, dst) yang menentukan hak akses.
Menu
Daftar menu/halaman yang tersedia lewat sistem RBAC dinamis.
RoleMenu
Tabel penghubung — menentukan menu mana yang boleh diakses oleh role mana.
CustomerDetails
Profil lengkap nasabah: NIK, alamat, foto KTP/KYC, dan kode referral miliknya.
DetailUserInternal
Profil staff internal (Checker/BM/Admin) — cabang tempat kerja dan no HP.
PasswordResetRequest
Antrean permintaan reset password staff yang perlu di-approve Superadmin.
Token
Token JWT (access & refresh) yang sedang aktif per user.
dbo — data referensi & wilayah cabang
Province / Regency / District / Village
Hierarki wilayah administratif Indonesia (provinsi → kab/kota → kecamatan → desa), dipakai untuk alamat nasabah dan lokasi cabang.
Branch
Data cabang klarFinance — tempat kerja BM.
BranchTerritory
Wilayah (kab/kota) yang jadi tanggung jawab tiap cabang — dasar filter antrean approval BM.
ApiParameter
API key per jenis client (Android/Angular) untuk validasi HMAC signature.
ParamGlobal
Parameter konfigurasi umum aplikasi (key-value).
los — scoring kredit & approval plafond
Vida
Hasil mock pengecekan KYC (verifikasi KTP/wajah dan penghasilan).
PefindoInquiry
Hasil mock pengecekan skor kredit/BI Checking (KOL).
LimitApplication
Pengajuan plafond nasabah — status dan jejak keputusan Checker → BM.
ApplicationLog
Riwayat/audit trail setiap tindakan pada satu pengajuan plafond.
ActiveLimit
Plafond yang sudah aktif/disetujui — total limit, terpakai, dan tersedia.
fin — pinjaman, cicilan & pembayaran
Drawdown
Catatan pencairan dana — lewat transfer bank atau QRIS.
LoanDetails
Rincian perhitungan bunga dan biaya dari satu pinjaman.
Loan
Pinjaman yang sedang berjalan — hasil pengajuan tunai, QRIS, atau tiket Transjakarta.
Installment
Cicilan bulanan per pinjaman beserta status bayarnya.
Repayment
Catatan tiap pembayaran cicilan yang masuk.
SavedBankAccount
Rekening tujuan pencairan yang tersimpan otomatis dari transaksi sebelumnya.
LoanReviewRequest
Pengajuan tunai yang membuat utilisasi plafond >30% — ditahan untuk direview ulang BM.
qris — pembayaran paylater (mockup)
Merchant
Data toko yang men-generate QR untuk menerima pembayaran QRIS.
QrisTransaction
Sesi transaksi QRIS dari scan sampai konfirmasi bayar.
referral — program "Ajak Teman"
ReferralInvite
Catatan siapa mengundang siapa lewat kode referral.
ReferralReward
Reward Rp100rb yang didapat referrer, siap dipakai otomatis ke pinjaman berikutnya.
transjakarta — tiket bus
TransjakartaTicket
Tiket bus yang dibeli nasabah — ditagih secara kolektif tiap bulan lewat satu Loan.
geo — konsen & pelacakan lokasi
LocationConsent
Status persetujuan nasabah untuk pelacakan lokasi.
LocationPing
Titik lokasi GPS yang dikirim berkala dari HP nasabah selama consent aktif.

Notes that don't fit in the boxes

Things the diagram implies but doesn't say outright.

auth
CustomerDetails.user_id and DetailUserInternal.user_id have no DB unique constraint — the 1:1 shown is convention (one profile per user), not enforced. Token holds both access and refresh tokens, not sessions.
dbo
Branch.id is a real DB SEQUENCE starting at 1000 (gap left for the pre-existing Head Office branch). One regency = max 1 Branch, except DKI Jakarta (max 2) — enforced in app code, not a DB constraint.
los
Two lock mechanisms, not one. BM's lock is the columns shown (permanent, no TTL). Checker's lock lives entirely in Redis (2-hour TTL, event-driven expiry) — LimitApplication has no column for it at all. Vida/PefindoInquiry are mock engines, not real integrations.
fin
Loan has no status column — active vs. paid-off is derived from whether any Installment is still UNPAID. Interest is flat-per-month by tenor (officially confirmed rates), not a placeholder.
qris
Mockup only — QR payload is just merchantCode, no EMVCo/BI network. Quota is min(30% × plafond, Rp1,000,000), cumulative, and independent from the unrelated ">30%" rule in fin.LoanReviewRequest.
referral
invitee_user_id is genuinely DB-unique. Reward (flat Rp100,000) goes to the referrer, not the invitee, and only fires once the invitee's own loan hits Rp1,000,000 — registration alone doesn't qualify.
transjakarta
The only place N children roll into 1 parent Loan by design — every ticket bought in a calendar month bills to one monthly Loan, due the 25th of the next month (every other Loan type uses rolling +30 days).
geo
LocationConsent.user_id is genuinely DB-unique and gates ping ingestion. Not shown: a read-only service clusters LocationPing rows into an inferred home/daytime location for Checker/BM/Superadmin — nothing inferred is persisted.
